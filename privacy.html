Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the rights you have over your data.

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Before this goes live: the bracketed fields below (legal entity name, registration number, registered address) need your real company details, and this document as a whole should get a pass from a lawyer before you rely on it as your actual privacy policy — this is a solid, accurate-to-the-product draft, not legal advice.

1. Who we are

[Vantly (Pty) Ltd — legal entity name], registration number [registration number], of [registered address, South Africa] ("Vantly", "we", "us") operates vantly.co.za and the Vantly application (together, the "Service"). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

2. What we collect

  • Account data — your email address and password (stored hashed, we never see it in plain text), via our authentication provider.
  • Brand Kit data — the brand colours, logo, voice and content you add to personalise the tools.
  • Content you create — copy, graphics, video and other output you generate or upload using the Service.
  • Usage data — which tools you use and how often, so we can improve the product and, if you opt in, surface performance insights back to you.
  • Billing data — if you're on a paid plan, our payment processor (Paystack) handles your card details directly; we never store your full card number.

3. What we don't do

  • We do not train AI models on your content or your Brand Kit.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not share your content with other customers.

4. Who we share data with

Only the processors that make the Service work: our hosting and database provider, our payment processor (Paystack) for billing, and the AI providers whose models generate your content (your inputs are sent to them to produce your outputs, under their own processing terms — never used by them to train on your behalf without consent).

5. Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and equivalent regimes elsewhere, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Email support@vantly.co.za and we'll action it within 30 days. You can also delete your account and content directly from Settings.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data and content within 30 days, except where we're required to keep billing records for tax/legal purposes.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production data is restricted to what's needed to operate and support the Service.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll email you and update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@vantly.co.za.
