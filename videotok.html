📝 Transcribe

Drop in a transcript (or transcribe a clip), and Volt repurposes it: YouTube titles, description, tags & chapters, a scroll-stopping TikTok / Reels caption, a full article (800+ words), or a ready-to-send email.

Extracts the audio and transcribes it — long sermons are auto-split into parts. Auto-detect works best for Setswana / Zulu / Xhosa and code-switching. If a format won't decode, it falls back to reading the audio in real time. You can always paste a transcript instead.
