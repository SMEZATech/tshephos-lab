Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vantly collects, uses and protects your personal information in accordance with POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) of South Africa.

1. Introduction

Vantly is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy covers the Vantly platform at vantly.co.za and app.vantly.co.za. By using our service, you agree to the collection and use of information described here.

2. Information We Collect

Account data

When you create an account: your email address, name (if provided), and password (stored as a secure hash — we never see it in plaintext).

Usage data

Pages visited, features used, time spent, and general performance metrics to help us understand what to improve.

Content data

Copy, graphics, videos, emails and other content you create. This is stored to power your workspace and belongs entirely to you.

Technical data

IP address, browser type, device type and operating system — collected automatically for security and functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing and maintaining your Vantly workspace
  • Improving features and fixing bugs
  • Sending account-related emails (security, billing, important updates)
  • Analysing aggregate usage to make better product decisions

We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.

4. AI & Your Data

Your content is never used to train AI models. Vantly sends prompts to third-party AI APIs (Google Gemini, Groq, and others) to generate outputs. We do not retain your prompts beyond your current session and never share content with AI providers for training purposes.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Supabase (EU region), encrypted at rest, and transmitted over TLS. We implement industry-standard security practices.

6. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers strictly necessary to operate the platform: Supabase (storage), AI API providers (prompt processing), Vercel (hosting), Stripe/Paystack (billing).

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use session cookies for authentication only. No third-party advertising trackers.

8. Data Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active plus 90 days after a deletion request.

9. Your Rights (POPIA)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your personal information. Email privacy@vantly.co.za to exercise these rights. We respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

Vantly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you by email at least 14 days before material changes take effect.

12. Contact

Email privacy@vantly.co.za or visit our Contact page.
